## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-16

### Added

- Local-only ZIP conversion through a loopback FastAPI interface.
- Safe nested-archive extraction with traversal, link, size, entry-count, path,
  and depth protections.
- Markdown-only result archives with a sanitised conversion report.
- Automatic upload and result cleanup.
- Double-click Mac launcher with pinned Python dependencies.
- API, archive, conversion, cleanup, browser, launcher, and real-format tests.
